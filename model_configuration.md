# openLCA Model Configuration

## Product

Heinz Tomato Ketchup 20 oz packaging case study.

Functional unit:

1 packaged 20 oz ketchup unit.

## LCA software

openLCA 2.6.1

## Impact method

TRACI 2.2

Impact category used in the current comparison:

Global Warming Potential

Unit:

kg CO2e per packaged unit

## System boundary

Screening cradle-to-gate packaging-material production.

Included:

- PET resin
- recycled PET resin
- PP resin
- containerboard proxy

Not currently included:

- label production
- bottle forming
- cap molding
- corrugated converting
- product filling
- distribution
- consumer use
- end-of-life

## Baseline packaging inputs

- PET bottle: 30 g
- PP cap: 4 g
- containerboard allocation: 18 g

Modeled packaging mass: 52 g/package.

The current label estimate is not included in LCIA because an appropriate inventory was not defined.
